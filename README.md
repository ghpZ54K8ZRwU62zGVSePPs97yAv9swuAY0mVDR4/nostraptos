# Nostr on Aptos

Implements Nostr protocols (including NIP-01) using Aptos framework and based on Aptos blockchain.

## Why Aptos

- Cost efficiency.
- Fast.
- Storage friendly (via Shelby).
- Bridges to DeFi and Bitcoin.

## How to build

- Schnorr signature scheme (on the roadmap).
- Aptos blockchain (of course).
- Nostr protocol (it's based off).
- Shelby (it's intended to use).

## What's the roadmap

- 2025 Q2: publish Nostr in Move to Rooch Network.
- 2025 Q3: publish Nostr on Aptos to Aptos blockchain.
- Future: publish Nostr on Sui to Sui blockchain.

## Who is behind

- The author of this project.
- Authors of Nostr protocol.
- Teams of Aptos.

## Where to publish

- Aptos (testnet and devnet).
- Sui (testnet and devnet).
- Rooch (mainnet, testnet and devnet).
